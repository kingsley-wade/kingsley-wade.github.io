This undergraduate project explored the problem from both directions. On the
mathematics side, we studied the dual of the Erdős–Ko–Rado theorem. On the
algorithms side, we designed a prime-based construction that outperforms
random forests in certain cases: we investigated $k$-subset selection problems
within $m$-element matrices where $m = p^2 + p + 1$ and $p$ is prime, extended
the construction to $p \times p$ matrices under the constraint $k + l = p$,
and further generalized it to $d$-dimensional vector-valued matrices.
